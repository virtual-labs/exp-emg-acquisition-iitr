<h3> Procedure for EMG Acquisition</h3>
<h4> 1. from Biceps (Elbow Flexion):</h4>



<b>Step 1: Establish connections</b><br>

1. Connect the E+ electrode on Biceps to the positive input (E+) of the op-amp circuit.<br>
2. Connect the E- electrode on Biceps to the negative terminal (E-) of first op-amp circuit.<br>
3. Connect the GRD electrode nearby bony landmark to the ground (GRD) of the op-amp circuit.<br>

<b>Step 2:</b>
Refer to the accompanying figure for correct connection points.<br>

<p align="center">
<img src="images/bicepsConnection.png" style="width:600px; margin-top:5px;">
</p>
<br>


<b>Step 3:</b>
Click the <b>Check</b> button to verify correct connections.<br>

<b>Step 4:</b>
 Click the <b>show signal</b> button to display the simulated EMG waveform.<br>

<b>Step 5:</b>
Click the <b>Normal</b> button to observe the baseline EMG signal when the muscle is relaxed. Then click the <b>Contraction</b> button to simulate muscle Contraction and observe the increase in EMG amplitude.

<b>Step 6:</b>
Click the <b>Signal</b> button to pause the displayed EMG waveform.<br>
 
<b>Step 7:</b>
Click the <b>Print</b> button to save the record observations.<br>
 
<b>Step 8:</b>
Select another muscle from the left panel to record EMG Signals for its corresponding muscle.
